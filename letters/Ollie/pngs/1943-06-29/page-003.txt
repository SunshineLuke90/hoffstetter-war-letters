If you told her that it would not
be any worse than a mosquito bite. She
must think that Inidia has some big mosquitos.
I am setting an a library and is it quiet.
There a silence card all over the place. That
is not for me.
Well time is up so I will close but not
for long. I have a history class today at 2.30 PM
I will see you then.
Well, here I am again in my good old
history. This class is good for letter writing only.
The all important day of the month is here tomorrow.
Pay Day!!!
Say I like the tag you put on the
back. Some stuff! You had the boys interested.
We have a soft ball game with section 3
to night. But with my ankle they
put me at water boy & score keeper.
I do have a ass't, I needed one. You
see we are in Flight B Section I. They call
us B-I or "Vita-man". What do you think
about it?
Well I have some other letters to
write so I will close.
Tell Pearl to write
Thanks for the letter and keep writing
Your friend
Ollie
