July 8, 1943
Dear Martha
Say, that was some
opening in that letter of yours.
This is the first letter that I
am not writing to you in class.
In fact I will never write
in class. The school wash me
out on low grades. But I do not
feel bad about it. In fact
I think I will love to get out of
school. I will send my new
address as fast as I get it.
I think I will go to
Jefferson Barrack. Well that
is enough of that.
I like that address
you put on the letter that
is pretty good. We got to more
games to play to win in flight B.
But they will be with out the help
of a great coach (me).
What is the matter don't you
get along with you roommate.
