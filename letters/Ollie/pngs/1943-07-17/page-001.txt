July 19, 1993
My Dearest Martha
I would like
to open my letter with a joke. The
Old jokes are the best. Like the
one about the man who, about
to be hanged, was asked if he
had anything to say and his
answer was: Yes, sir, I want to
say this is going to be a lesson to me
If you do not like the opening tell
me, please take it easy.
I am sitting writing this letter
I do not know why because the other
boys have gone to a big parade. And
if there is anything I like is a parade with
a band. But I feel a lot better
when I write to you. Boy I should
do not know why because before
I got in the army I never wrote a
letter in my life. But I real enjoy
getting one from you (letters I mean).
Do you know I get more letters
from you then any body in St. Louis.
