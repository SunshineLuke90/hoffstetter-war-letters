July 21, 1943
Dear Martha
Well how is the old home?
I can just see you sitting down reading
this letter with a pair of shorts on and
a nice big glass of ice tea
with a little sugar
in it. and look at the chair I bet
it is the softest chair in the house.
What say? how right am I.
Hold tight I got some jokes
(I think they are).
"She used to be my flame until
she went out with that squirt"
Or how about this one.
"Isn't this wonderful sitting here
before the fireplace? Isn't that a
swell fire!"
"Yeh, but I'm sure going to miss
the furniture"
I could not find a picture of
myself at home. There was all the
time some body with me. But I
will put one in this letter.
