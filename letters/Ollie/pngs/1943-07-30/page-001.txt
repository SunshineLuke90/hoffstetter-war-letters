July 30, 1943
Dear Martha
I just got you picture and
I can not thank you enought for it.
I think you look very beautiful. In
your letter you said that you were
going to put the picture in the letter that
is why I through I lost it. But now
I am very happy to get the picture.
I do not have much news. I try to
get out of fire guard or K.P. They just
love to put me on K.P.
I went to the opera last night
in convoy there were 3 truck and 1 had
WAAC in it. I was a nice boy I
did not even talk to them. and I
meet some girl I knew in High school.
She is in nursing. She as her vacation
but it is only for 2 weeks. So you
see you are very lucky to get 3 weeks.
