

Aug 31-
Dear Martha,
I just got you letter when
I was on K.P. and one of my friends brought
it to me at noon. Boy it shure was a great
help. I was very happy to get a long letter
+ I like that paper boy was that
from to look at. And if you have the
"c" card well, I can get the car but
not the gas. I shure like the writing
on the back of the letter. That was
O.S.E. all that is missing is the stamp
being put on up side down + S.W.A.K.
say when am I going to get that
from the last letter you mailed
Speaking of shoe rationing, the other
night a guy pushed me in a doorway;
pulled a gun + said,  "Put up your feet"!
If you do not like that one how about
this,
