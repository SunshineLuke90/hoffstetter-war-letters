Aut. 4
Dear Martha:
I am very
sorry about the paper +
how this may look, my
handwriting. We
are out at Forest Park
were that army is
to put on a big battle
Sunday. It is for
the picture "This
is the army" which
will soon come out.
So that will make
me a movie star.
We are to put this fight
on at Art hill you
may know where
that is.
