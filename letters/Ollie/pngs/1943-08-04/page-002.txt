Just to show you
how it is out here
I well try my hand
at drawing. Do no
laugh to much.
We started out here with
a rifle on which I am
now writing, a pack
in which is our mess
cup and spoons, fork, knife
a raincoat, a blanket
part of a tent and some
soap + tooth brush.
all this with a gas mask
battle helmets.
that was vain
the guard patrol just
went pass. Boy
is this fun in this small
tent without a pillow.
