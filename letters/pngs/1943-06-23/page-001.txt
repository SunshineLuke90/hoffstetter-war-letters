Juune 23, 1943
Helle Martha.
I was on my furlough
when you letter came, so I could not answer
you letter before. It will take me about
two weeks to get all the sleep I need.
I sleep in just about all my closs monday,
& Tuesday. More fun! I am writing this
letter in history so I do not go to sleep.
I was glad to here my letter was
so welcome. I was very glad to here
from you.
The test we had were difficutt
but I did not spoil my furlough.
And I enjoy my furlough very much.
But the monday we got back our
Gym work, just about killed us. Boy
one week with out it and you are all out
of condition.
When you said that the soldier
came to capping the other night. Is that
capping when you got you caps. I think that
is what Pearl said.
I am glad you meet some soldier you
like.
