June 29, 1943
What Say Maitha.
I was very happy to
get you letter. I did not think you would
find time to answer so soon. Thanks a lot.
Sorry I run out of the other pages. You
see this is the time I should be doing my
excercise. It looks like I write you only when
I whould be dong sonething else. The reason
I have for not exercising is that I sprained
my ankle in a hot game get this of volley ball.
I play football all year and nothing happens, then
I play a girl games and I am out for a week.
they got a nice looking nurse at the
hospital so it is not to bad. What do you
say about that?
I tryed to listen to the teacher to-day
but I feel asleep. So I just as soon write letters.
What I get out of that history class you can put
in a pin head. There is just some little thing
come up monday a history test. More fun.
I will have to go on the "admiral" some
day from what you said about it and the
boys in the room went on it Sat. Boy were
the feeling good. In fact one does not
remember what happen to him from 11:00
on. We had one hell (sorry abolut the mouth)
of a time trying to get him to bed.
I hope you do not come home like that.
But the story they have about the gals well
they all got dates the week.
Boy I bet it feels good not to study in the
time you have off. If you did study at all.
Please to not get mad for for what I said.
Don't you know how to take I in baths
now. I was good to asked you what I should
do with my ankle, but I did not need it cut
off.
I died laughing at the part in your letter
about the typo you gave to the lady.
