"A Jewish Merchant was
dozing, and momma, his two
daughters and three sons were
at the bedside saying the raddish.
Finally momma bent over her
departing spouse and sobbed:
Can you hear, pappa? Well are
all here - all your daughters
and sons and old momma,
praying for you."
"Is Milton here?" whispered the
dying man.
"Yes, pappa."
"Is Moe here?"
"Yes, Pappa."
"Is Abe here?"
"Yes, Pappa."
With a scream of anguish pappa
sat up in bed. "Then who in hells'
looking after the store?"
