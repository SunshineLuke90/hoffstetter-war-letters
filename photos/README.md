## Requirements

Display
--
The photos should display as cards, tiling the page dynamically.
Photos should optionally have an option to flip over. Not all photos will have a flip option available. The flip should display if the photo folder has both a front.jpg and a back.jpg. For the photo flip design, the photo would be hovering over the top portion of the card, and then flip over itself, without adjusting the sizing of the card itself. The subject & date would be rendered as text, below the image, but within the card.

Search
--
Photos should have some metadata.

- Accessible description of the photo
  ex: Two men, in army attire, standing outside in an army barracks
- Subject (not just the accessible description)
  ex: Ollie and Overton in the barracks
- Date
